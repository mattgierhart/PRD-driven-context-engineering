## Group observations

**These two skills are the "define success, then judge it honestly" pair of v0.9, and their strongest material is judgment, not process.** The richest distilled-experience concentration in the group is `validation-criteria.md` — the go/no-go rubric, A-F grade, pivot-vs-kill taxonomy, and Week 1 early-warning thresholds form a complete, deterministic launch adjudicator that maps almost 1:1 onto the v2 `check` + policy-pack model with the Change plane receiving pivot/kill proposals. Second is the reconciliation table in the gtm-strategy SKILL.md — the only cross-framework consistency check in the whole v0.9 suite, and it would be silently lost in a naive "delete the orchestrator, keep the sub-skills" consolidation because it lives in the file most likely to be judged "just routing."

**Cross-skill patterns**: (1) The six-type product taxonomy (v0.2) is the connective tissue — it resurfaces here as product-type→channel, product-type→messaging, and product-type→metric-calibration mappings, in three separately-maintained copies. In v2 these should be one taxonomy table in the Intent plane that playbooks read, not restate. (2) Pivot triggers exist in three places (launch-strategies.md, gtm-template.md, validation-criteria.md) with slightly different wording — consolidate into the `launch-validation` policy pack as the single authority. (3) Both skills duplicate their own content internally (gtm-template.md restates all three references; launch-metrics SKILL.md prints its four examples twice) — classic small-context insurance, pure token cost now.

**Consolidation opportunities**: gtm-strategy already models the v2 target — an orchestrator whose sub-skills are framework playbooks — so the v2 move is to finish the job: fold orchestration into the `shape` kernel verb, promote the reconciliation table and validation-criteria into two policy packs (`gtm-coherence`, `launch-validation`), merge the benchmark-heavy references (channel economics, funnel rates, launch-strategy gates) into a shared benchmarks reference library with single ownership, and retire both fill-in-the-blank mega-templates after harvesting their few distilled pockets (pivot triggers, red-zone playbooks, product-type implication one-liners). Also fix or drop launch-metrics' dangling "Detailed References" pointers — evidence that reference packaging has already drifted once without detection.
