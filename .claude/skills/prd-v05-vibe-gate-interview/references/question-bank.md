# Vibe Gate Question Bank

Phase-organized questions. Operating contract: one question at a time, always offer your own
suggested answer, decide (and flag at 1/5) on "I don't know".

## Opening (show the map + confidence dial)

- "This is a gut-check, not a second red-team. Seven short beats: framing, experience, your
  riskiest bet, honest cost, first ten users, then a verdict."
- "Before we start — how battle-tested is the evidence behind this plan? Mostly interviews
  and data, or mostly your read of the market?" *(sets pacing; don't re-derive Tier-1 evidence)*

## Phase 2 — Framing Check

- "Be honest with me: did this start from a problem you found, or from 'I want to build X'?"
- "If this ships exactly as specced, does the v0.1 outcome actually move? Walk me from the
  top three FEA- entries to the KPI they're supposed to push."
- "Which load-bearing piece of evidence are you least sure of?" *(check its confidence score)*
- "Look at the needs we wrote down — is any of them secretly a feature wearing a need's
  clothes?"

## Phase 3 — Experience Gut-Checks

**Grandma Test**
- "Who's the least techy person who'd ever use this? Could THEY do everything we just
  described with nobody helping? If not, what has to get simpler?"

**Stress Test**
- "Picture your user at their most stressed, most distracted. Low battery. Bad signal. Kid
  screaming. Running late. Walk me through them trying to use this in THAT moment. Where
  does it fall apart?"

**Aha-Moment Timing**
- "What's the single moment a user first feels this was worth it?"
- "How fast can they get there after signing up? Aim for the first 30 seconds — what's
  standing between signup and that moment that we could strip?"

## Phase 4 — Riskiest-Assumption Test

- "Name the single belief that sinks the whole thing if it's wrong. Just one — if you give
  me two, you haven't decided."
- "What's the cheapest way to check that BEFORE building? Landing page with a waitlist, ten
  DMs to people with the problem, a fake-door button, a rough mock shown to five people?"
- Rule to say out loud: "If the test takes two weeks to set up, it's a project, not a test."
- "When will the check have run — give me a date I can write down."

## Phase 5 — Honesty Pass

- "On a 1–10 where a to-do list is a 2 and Instagram is a 9 — where is this? Here's my
  read: [score], because [TECH-/ARC- scope]."
- "Which service in the stack could surprise you on cost — not its price tag, but how we'd
  *use* it? (Polling every 30 seconds vs. getting pinged is $480/month vs. free.)"
- "Honest timeline, in phases — what's V1, what's parked?"
- "Is this for learning, or for real users? The answer changes how much we sweat quality,
  testing, and legal before v0.6."

## Phase 6 — Distribution Final Boss

- "Who are your first 10 users, *specifically*? Not a demographic — ten real people or one
  real place you could name today. 'The folks in r/[subreddit] who keep ranting about X'
  counts. 'Small business owners' does not."
- "Where do they already gather? The single place they're hanging out having this problem
  out loud."
- "What's your first move to reach them? One concrete action."
- Blunt gut-check, said out loud: "If you can't name where the first ten users come from,
  that isn't a distribution problem for later. It's the riskiest part of this whole thing,
  and it deserves more attention than another feature."

## Phase 7 — Verdict

- "Here's my read of the seven beats and my suggested verdict — open the decision sheet I
  just generated, click what YOU decide, and paste the block back to me."
- On PAUSE: "What, concretely, would have to be true for this to flip to GO? That's the
  unblock condition we write down."
