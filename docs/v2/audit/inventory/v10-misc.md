## Group observations

**Cross-skill patterns.** This group is the v1.0 evidence-and-social-proof cluster plus two infrastructure pieces. The four customer-facing skills form a deliberate pipeline: Moore defines *who* (beachhead, ADO-REF candidates) → Torres defines *what to learn* (OST) → Mom Test defines *how to learn it* (interview discipline + confidence tiers) → Case Study/Testimonial convert the accumulated evidence into Delivery-plane assets. The connective tissue (ADO-REF lifecycle, in-segment-only rule, consent management, "their voice" editing rule) is restated in 2–4 places each — classic small-context-model duplication that v2's plane model eliminates: state each rule once on the plane schema or in a policy pack, and let playbooks reference it.

**Strongest distilled-experience material.** (1) The Mom Test **confidence calibration ladder** — mapping evidence types to the 2/5–5/5 P4 scale is methodology-original, makes interview quality machine-checkable, and should be promoted from playbook content to kernel Evidence-plane schema. (2) Moore's **stage-indicator diagnostic table + numeric thresholds** ("flat MRR despite growing leads", >70%/>50% cuts) — the book gives the theory; this gives the audit instrument. (3) The testimonial **six-trigger taxonomy with working ask scripts**. (4) The **consent-scope schema with original-text audit trail** (shared by both proof skills). (5) The anti-pattern tables throughout are consistently the highest-density operator knowledge per token — "pragmatists smell failures-rebranded-as-wins", "anonymous testimonials get discounted", "beachhead is tighter than Dunford best-fit", "market-size gets Low weight".

**Consolidation opportunities.** (a) Merge case-study-builder + testimonial-collector into one `build --playbook=social-proof` family with a shared `social-proof` policy pack (consent, voice, segment-fit, quarterly refresh) — roughly 40% of their combined text is duplicated rules. (b) Split Moore into `learn --playbook=adoption-stage` (Reality diagnostic, cheap and re-runnable) and `decide --playbook=crossing-the-chasm` (Intent commitment) — the skill already has this seam at Step 1 vs Steps 2–5. (c) Torres + Mom Test stay separate playbooks (`learn --playbook=continuous-discovery`, `explore --playbook=mom-test`) but drop their mutual restatements; the OST deserves consideration as a first-class kernel artifact bridging Evidence→Change since opportunities are proposals-in-waiting. (d) `init` is already v2-shaped (thin wrapper over a deterministic manifest-driven script) and should be the packaging model for the whole kernel. (e) SKILL_TEMPLATE retires into the playbook-registry authoring template, carrying only its anti-Goodhart footnote and required-section anatomy. Model-era scaffolding to strip everywhere: per-skill execution-mode tables (keep only mode-specific *deliverable* definitions, since `--depth` survives), "Position in workflow" breadcrumbs (become the journey doc), and Consumes/Produces ID plumbing (becomes declared plane reads/writes).
