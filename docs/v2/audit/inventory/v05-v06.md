## Group observations

**Cross-skill patterns.** (1) Every SKILL.md in this group carries its template and worked examples 2-3 times — once in "Produces", once in "Output Template", once in assets/ or references/ — pure small-context-model insurance that accounts for roughly 40-50% of token mass and can collapse to a single registry-held template per artifact type. (2) The same meta-discipline recurs under three names: risk-closure (every RISK→TECH), architecture-closure (every high RISK→ARC), and contract-closure (every SCR/UJ→API→DBT, no orphans). This is one idea — *upstream nodes must have downstream responses, and no downstream node may lack an upstream justification* — and it is exactly what the devgraph/readiness machinery already checks at v0.7. It should be one kernel concept with per-stage policy-pack instantiations, not three hand-rolled matrices. (3) Every skill ends with a Quality Gates checklist that duplicates ghm-gate-check territory — these are the raw material for named policy packs.

**Strongest distilled-experience material in this group** (the richness worth guarding during consolidation): the risk question bank and the status-weighted scoring model with accepted=1.0; the Reuse-first build/buy taxonomy with the brownfield 80% rule and strong-signal phrase table; the MVP cost stance with actual dollar figures ("$200-300/mo with good DX beats $50/mo with constant ops"); the conformance-rule mechanism turning ARC- decisions into machine-checkable topology; the CLI-over-MCP doctrine; the contract-closure/orphan rubric; and the anti-pattern tables throughout, which are the densest per-token operator knowledge (risk theater caps, "diagram without decisions", N+1-baked-in, resume-driven development, 70%-confidence timeboxing).

**Consolidation opportunities.** The v0.5→v0.6 chain (RISK → TECH → ARC → API/DBT → ENV) is one continuous traceability thread and reads naturally as a single guided-journey doc section ("from risks to contracts"), with four playbooks behind it: `explore --playbook=red-team-interview`, `decide --playbook=build-buy-reuse`, `shape --playbook=architecture-adr`, `shape --playbook=api-contracts`. Environment-setup dissolves into kernel `build`/`init` behavior plus a one-page CLI-over-MCP reference. The five anti-pattern tables should merge into a shared anti-pattern library with de-duplication ("design for 10x not 1000x" currently lives in two skills verbatim). Scoring assets (risk status-weights, tech evaluation scorecard) and the closure rubrics belong in policy packs consumed by `check`, which preserves their deterministic, LLM-free character — consistent with the repo's own Anti-Goodhart rule.
