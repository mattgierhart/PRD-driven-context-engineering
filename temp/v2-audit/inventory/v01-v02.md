## Group observations

**Cross-skill pattern (the packaging tax).** All four skills share an identical chassis: Consumes/Produces ceremony → numbered step workflow → tier rubric → anti-pattern table → quality gate checklist → research prompts → copy-paste templates → examples. Every template and rubric is duplicated 2-3 times (SKILL.md inline, assets/ copy, sometimes references/ again) — pure small-context insurance where the model couldn't be trusted to open a second file. In v2, each rubric/template needs exactly one home.

**The tier system is kernel, not playbook.** The group runs *three deliberate variants* of one evidence philosophy — pain-evidence tiers (v01-pf), value-desire tiers (v01-uva), underserved-segment signals (v02-clm), plus a fourth grading in v02-ptc — all descending from "what people spend money on > what they build workarounds for > what they quantify > what they say > what we assume," with an explicit reject tier and a mandatory "would move to X/5 if…" upgrade condition. This is the Evidence plane's native schema: tier + confidence + upgrade-condition should be fields on every Evidence-plane record, with the per-context variants preserved as a small rubric library. Do not flatten the variants into one — the pain/desire distinction is real.

**Strongest distilled-experience material in the group**, in order: (1) the entire prd-v02-product-type-classification reference set — six-type taxonomy, GTM constraints matrix with hard numbers, graduated confidence thresholds, confidence-boost priors, and real misclassification postmortems; (2) the evidence-tier + upgrade-condition system; (3) the 1% Better Hypothesis discipline (explicit counter-programming against 10x founder inflation, with kill criteria); (4) the current-behavior-first ordering rule; (5) the framing-type-from-verbatim-language decision matrix in value articulation.

**Consolidation opportunities.** (a) Merge the two v0.1 skills into one `explore/shape --playbook=spark` pair or a single problem-and-value playbook — they share fixtures, chassis, and a single gate. (b) Extract all four research-prompts.md files into one research-recipe library keyed by gap type / tier target (the recipes are the group's most reusable craft: salary-as-budget-proxy, 1-3-star review mining, template-download and freelance-rate demand proxies, churn-signal searches). (c) The landscape→classification pair is one continuous explore→decide arc; the classification's GTM constraints matrix becomes a policy pack so `check` can enforce type guardrails on v0.3+ decisions instead of relying on downstream skills re-reading prose. (d) CFD/BR entry formats belong to the memory-plane schema definitions, not to any playbook. Nothing in this group qualifies for retire — even the worksheet's scaffolding wraps two distilled checks (hybrid-type driver, anti-pattern verification) that must migrate into the decide verb's default behavior.
