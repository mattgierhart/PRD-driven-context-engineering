The Source-of-Truth Review · № 01 — Atlas & Index

Source-of-Truth Atlas

The human-readable face of the knowledge graph. Every durable decision in this product has a unique ID; every ID has exactly one canonical owning surface; every reference is a hyperlink. This page is the map — pick the view that matches the question you're asking.

Uninitialized synthetic template. The numbered cards in these companion pages demonstrate review formats only. They become product truth only after their Markdown source replaces the examples, sets template_state: active, and is accepted in the PRD gate snapshot.

Who reads this page: anyone entering the graph — founder, new teammate, or reviewing agent. It complements SoT.UNIQUE_ID_SYSTEM.md (governance) and SoT.README.md (library guide).
