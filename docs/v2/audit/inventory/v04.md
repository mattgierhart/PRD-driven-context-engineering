## Group observations

**This is one pipeline, not four skills.** PER → UJ → SCR/DES → prototype is a single continuous transformation of Evidence+Intent into a reviewable Delivery-plane design, sharing one worked example (the Ops-Manager reporting SaaS) threaded consistently through all four — that continuity is itself an asset and argues for a single `shape` playbook family (or one `experience-design` playbook with phases) rather than four registry entries. The shared skeleton (Consumes/Produces + confidence boilerplate + anti-pattern table + quality gates + downstream table) is ~40% of each SKILL.md and is almost entirely model-era scaffolding the kernel should own once.

**Strongest distilled-experience material in the group**: (a) the numeric operating limits — 5 personas max, <15 MVP pages, >8 prototype screens = scope creep, 3-5 templates to avoid decision paralysis, 3 review cycles max, Stitch 60-70% first-gen accuracy — these are exactly the numbers a fresh model won't produce; (b) the **bidirectional coverage matrices** (feature↔journey, feature↔screen) that turn scope pruning into a mechanical orphan check with a forced add-or-cut decision — this is the group's best candidate for a `check` policy pack because it's deterministic; (c) the **Negative persona** and **Money Shot** concepts — small named ideas with outsized leverage (one prevents wasted sales effort, the other bridges design to GTM); (d) the **emotional-beat mapping** and the **3-cycle upstream diagnostic**, which encode judgment about when design iteration is failing and why.

**Consolidation opportunities**: (1) Merge the two overlapping tool-knowledge files (design-tool-examples.md, tool-adaptation-notes.md) into one design-tool adapter reference — they're two snapshots of the same knowledge from different tool eras; refresh names, keep the selection logic. (2) Promote all coverage/gate checklists (persona evidence links, journey KPI links, feature↔journey↔screen coverage, dead-end nav, screen caps, prototype-review evidence) into a single **v0.4-exit policy pack** for `check`. (3) The PER/UJ/SCR/DES entry templates become Delivery-plane schemas, not skill prose — each currently exists in 2-3 places per skill. (4) The feedback-capture disposition flow generalizes beyond prototypes and should become kernel `learn`/Change-plane behavior. (5) design-brief-guidance.md is the only file that reads like undigested legacy material — harvest Critical Moments and the quality thresholds, retire the rest.
