## Group observations

- **This pair is the operational-numbers vault of the methodology.** Unlike upstream discovery skills, nearly every asset here carries hard numerators: 14.4x/6x burn rates, 43.2 min/month, 50/75/90/100% budget ladder, <5 min decide / <15 min execute, 25%-of-users severity band, 30-day flag-removal rule, 24–48h blue retention, per-nine cost multipliers. These numbers are exactly what a fresh model invents *vaguely* ("set appropriate thresholds") — they are the distilled experience the owner wants preserved, and they should survive verbatim in v2 playbook references.
- **Strongest single artifact in the group**: slo-guide.md (error-budget math + burn-rate derivation + budget-policy-to-org-behavior ladder + cost-per-nine table). Second: rollback-procedures.md's decision tree + rollback-culture framing. Third: examples.md's annotated bad deployment plans — the ✅/❌ teaching format is worth adopting as a house style for all v2 playbook example sets.
- **The two skills are one system narrated twice.** DEP- rollback triggers ARE MON- alert conditions; DEP- baselines ARE MON- baselines; both restate the other's entries in their own examples. In v2 the duplication collapses naturally if thresholds live once as Delivery-plane data (a DEP/MON entry pair sharing one threshold record) and both playbooks read it. Proposed consolidation: `build --playbook=release-engineering`, `build --playbook=observability-setup`, shared `slo-error-budget` + `rollback-response` reference libraries, and two check policy packs (`release-ready`, `monitoring-ready`) whose criteria are already written as Quality Gates checklists in both SKILL.md files.
- **Reality-plane note for v2**: monitoring-setup is the skill that *defines the Reality plane's schema* (what telemetry exists, what "normal" is), and release-planning's rollback triggers are the first Reality→Change adjudication rules in the lifecycle. Worth making that explicit in the v2 blueprint — these aren't just "ops docs", they're the plumbing between Delivery, Reality, and Change.
- **Drift/scaffolding cleanups**: duplicated example blocks in both SKILL.md files (small-context reinforcement), two dead reference links in release-planning's SKILL.md (`environment-examples.md`, `rollback-guide.md`), and a DEP- type-system mismatch between SKILL.md and dep-template.md. The tool-comparison half of monitoring-stack.md is the only material in the group safe to retire/regenerate — but keep its stage/budget bands and validation checklist.
